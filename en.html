Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

1. Who provides the app?

The Breeder’s Journal (Oppdretterens Kulldagbok) is developed and provided by Carl Fredrik Lilliedahl Jenzer Bålsrød. For privacy questions, contact carl.fredrik1997@yahoo.com.

2. Information you enter

The app lets you record and organize information related to dog breeding, including kennel name, parent dogs, litters and puppies, health tests, mating, temperature, progesterone, birth, weight, reminders, photos, documents and free-text notes.

Information about animals is generally not personal data. Photos, documents, kennel names and free-text notes may, however, contain information about people. You are responsible for ensuring that information you enter may be processed lawfully.

3. Local storage and iCloud

Records, photos and attachments are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, app data is also transferred to The Breeder’s Journal’s private CloudKit storage associated with your Apple Account. Attachments may be stored as separate CloudKit records. File names, file types, file sizes and checksums may be used to manage and verify transfers.

Sync takes place while the app is open, active and connected to the internet. The journal content itself is not sent to a separate server operated by the developer. Apple provides iCloud and CloudKit. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo in the app. Photo Library access is used when you choose an existing image. Notifications are used for reminders and breeder tips. Permissions can be changed in iPhone or iPad settings. Features that require a permission may then become unavailable.

5. Sync between devices

When iCloud sync is enabled, changes to records and attachments can be transferred between your devices using the same Apple Account. The app does not sync in the background; it must be open and active.

If multiple devices contain conflicting changes, the app may stop syncing and ask you how to handle the conflict instead of automatically overwriting data.

6. Backups

You can manually create a complete .kulldagbok backup. It may contain your records, photos and attachments. The backup is a separate file and you choose where to store it. The file is not encrypted by the app. Store it securely and delete copies you no longer need.

iCloud sync is not the same as a separate .kulldagbok backup.

7. Deleting local data

The “Reset app data” function deletes the app’s records, photos and attachments from the current device and turns off sync on that device. This action does not delete data that remains in iCloud, and it does not delete .kulldagbok backups stored elsewhere.

8. Deleting all iCloud data

Under “Sync between devices”, you can choose “Delete all iCloud data”. The function first stops iCloud sync on the current device and then deletes The Breeder’s Journal’s stored CloudKit data, including kennel data and synchronized photos and attachments, from iCloud.

Local data on the device from which you perform the deletion is retained. After successful deletion, iCloud sync remains turned off on that device. The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you use The Breeder’s Journal on multiple devices, you should repeat the deletion on the other devices. This reduces the risk that another device later attempts to sync its local data back to iCloud.

9. How long is information stored?

The app does not apply an automatic expiry period to your journal content. Data remains locally and, if enabled, in iCloud until you edit or delete it, reset the app, use the iCloud deletion function, or otherwise remove it.

The developer cannot restore journal content that no longer exists on your devices, in iCloud or in an available backup.

10. TestFlight and feedback

If you use a TestFlight version of the app, Apple processes testing-related information and may make crash data, usage information and submitted feedback available to the developer. Your name and email address may be available depending on how you were invited to the test. Screenshots or text that you submit as feedback may contain information from the app.

Such information is used only for testing, troubleshooting, support and improving the app. Information you send directly to the developer may be retained for as long as necessary to handle the request, document an issue or meet legal obligations, and is deleted when no longer needed. Apple’s processing of TestFlight data is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy rules. See TestFlight and Privacy.

11. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell personal data and does not use your journal content for advertising. Apple processes data when you use iCloud, CloudKit, the App Store or TestFlight in accordance with Apple’s own terms and privacy rules.

12. Your rights

For personal data you send directly to the developer, you may contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion in accordance with applicable privacy law. The developer does not have access to enter your private journal or Apple Account to edit or restore content there.

If you believe your personal data is being processed unlawfully, you may contact the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or another competent supervisory authority.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s functionality or data processing changes. The date at the top shows when the policy was last updated.
